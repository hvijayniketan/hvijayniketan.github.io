Hotel near Glass Bridge Rajgir: Why Stay at Hotel Vijay

The Glass Bridge is a glass-floor skywalk inside Rajgir Nature Safari, one of the most popular things to do with family and friends in Rajgir.

Hotel Vijay is about 2 km from the Rajgir Wildlife Safari. Ask our front desk for the current route, timings and ticket advice before you go.

Combine it with the Griddhakuta ropeway and hot springs. See our Glass Bridge and Nature Safari package.

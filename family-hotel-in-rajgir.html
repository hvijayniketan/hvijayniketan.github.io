Hotel Vijay, Rajgir

Family Hotel in Rajgir

Family Rooms with two beds and AC, near safari, ropeway and the hot springs.

Hotel Vijay offers Family Rooms with 2 beds, AC, LED TV, extra space and Wi-Fi. Free parking, a restaurant with room service and a 24/7 front desk make travel with children and elders easier.

Rajgir is full of family outings: the ropeway, wildlife safari, Nature Safari with the glass bridge and Ghora Katora Lake.

Nearby places

Rajgir Wildlife Safari

2 km from hotel

A forest safari area close to town where families can spot wildlife. Children usually love it.

Rajgir Ropeway

approx. 4 km from hotel

The easy way up to the Peace Pagoda. A short ride with views over green hills, ideal for elders and children.

Nature Safari and Glass Bridge

approx. 9 km from hotel

An 85-foot glass skywalk high above the valley, set inside a nature park with a suspension bridge, adventure zone and butterfly zone.

Ghora Katora Lake

approx. 9 km from hotel

A scenic lake with Mahabharata-era legends, surrounded by hills. A good picnic and sunset stop.

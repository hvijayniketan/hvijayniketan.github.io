Hotel Vijay, Rajgir

Rajgir Tourist Places and Distances

Everything worth seeing around Rajgir, with approximate distance from Hotel Vijay.

Hotel Vijay is on Hot Spring Road, so many sacred and historic sites are close by. Distances are approximate road distances from the hotel; confirm travel time on Google Maps.

Walking distance and nearbyUnder 3 km from the hotel

Sri Ramkrishna Sarada Ashram

50 ft from hotel

A calm Ramakrishna Mission centre right beside the hotel. Guests enjoy its peaceful prayers and spiritual atmosphere, a rare quiet spot in a busy tourist town.

Rajgir Hot Springs (Brahmakund)

1 km from hotel

Sacred springs fed by the seven streams of Saptadhara, with separate bathing areas for men and women. Pilgrims believe the water heals, and the springs matter to Hindus, Buddhists and Jains alike.

Venuvan (Bamboo Grove)

1 km from hotel

A tranquil bamboo garden remembered as the grove King Bimbisara offered to the Buddha. Good for a slow morning walk.

Lakshmi Narayan Temple and Saptaparni Cave trail

approx. 1 km from hotel

The temple stands at the foot of the hill. A 40-minute uphill hike from behind it leads to the Saptaparni Caves, tied to early Buddhist tradition.

Jarasandh Ki Baithak (Pippala Cave)

approx. 1.5 km from hotel

A rock structure above the hot springs on Vaibhava Hill, used as a watch tower and later by hermits. It carries the name of King Jarasandh of the Mahabharata.

Rajgir Wildlife Safari

2 km from hotel

A forest safari area close to town where families can spot wildlife. Children usually love it.

Famous Rajgir tourist placesWithin about 10 km

Vishwa Shanti Stupa (Peace Pagoda)

4.4 km from hotel

Rajgir's most famous landmark: a marble stupa built by Japanese Buddhists on a hilltop, with four golden Buddha statues and sweeping views of the valley.

Rajgir Ropeway

approx. 4 km from hotel

The easy way up to the Peace Pagoda. A short ride with views over green hills, ideal for elders and children.

Griddhakuta (Vulture's Peak)

approx. 5 km from hotel

One of Buddhism's holiest sites, where the Buddha is said to have given many important teachings.

Bimbisara Jail

approx. 5 km from hotel

The ancient site where King Bimbisara is said to have been imprisoned by his son, with a view toward Griddhakuta.

Son Bhandar Caves

approx. 4 km from hotel

Two rock-cut caves from the 3rd-4th century with early Jain carvings and an undeciphered shell-script inscription. Legend links them to Bimbisara's lost treasure.

Swarn Bhandar and Maniyar Math

approx. 4 km from hotel

A well-like archaeological structure with a small Jain shrine, found on the way to the Son Bhandar caves.

Cyclopean Wall

approx. 6 km from hotel

Remains of a massive stone wall that once ringed ancient Rajgir for about 40 km. It is among the oldest stone fortifications in India.

Nature Safari and Glass Bridge

approx. 9 km from hotel

An 85-foot glass skywalk high above the valley, set inside a nature park with a suspension bridge, adventure zone and butterfly zone.

Ghora Katora Lake

approx. 9 km from hotel

A scenic lake with Mahabharata-era legends, surrounded by hills. A good picnic and sunset stop.

Day trips from RajgirNalanda and beyond

Nalanda Mahavihara (UNESCO World Heritage Site)

15 km from hotel

Ruins of the ancient Buddhist university that drew scholars from across Asia. Pair it with the Nalanda Archaeological Museum and Xuanzang Memorial Hall.

Bihar Sharif

approx. 25 km from hotel

A historic town known for the shrine of the Sufi saint Makhdum Sharfuddin and its old mosques.

Pawapuri (Jal Mandir)

approx. 38 km from hotel

A major Jain pilgrimage site where Lord Mahavira is believed to have attained nirvana.

Bodh Gaya (Mahabodhi Temple)

approx. 85 km from hotel

The place of the Buddha's enlightenment. Many guests combine it with Rajgir on a Buddhist circuit tour.

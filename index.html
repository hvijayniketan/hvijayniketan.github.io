Our Story

A Home in the Valley of Rajgir

Nestled in the serene valleys of Rajgir — one of India's most sacred and historically rich destinations — Hotel Vijay offers warm, comfortable hospitality at the heart of this ancient city.

Located beside the Ram Krishna Math on Hot Spring Road, our hotel sits at an exotic vantage point offering breathtaking views of sunsets and sunrises cradled in nature's lap. Whether you are a pilgrim, a history enthusiast, or a leisure traveller, we promise a stay that is simple, honest, and memorable.

With dedicated service and homelike warmth, Hotel Vijay has made every journey to Rajgir truly unforgettable — from the sacred hot springs to the Nalanda ruins and the Vishwa Shanti Stupa.

Bihar is a dry state. Liquor and all intoxicants are strictly prohibited at Hotel Vijay, in rooms, in common areas, in parking and in vehicles on our premises.

Liquor Policy (Bihar Prohibition)

Under the Bihar Prohibition and Excise Act, 2016, it is an offence to manufacture, bottle, distribute, transport, store, possess, buy, sell or consume liquor or any intoxicating substance anywhere in Bihar. This also applies to guests from other states and countries, even if liquor is legal where you live.

What this means for our guests

  • We do not serve, sell, arrange or allow liquor, and we do not allow other intoxicants or drugs.
  • Do not bring liquor or intoxicants into the hotel, even sealed bottles in luggage or vehicles.
  • Persons who appear drunk or under the influence may be refused entry or check-in.
  • Do not ask our staff to bring, buy or arrange liquor from outside.

If this policy is broken

  • The guest may be asked to leave immediately. No refund is given for the booking.
  • We must follow the law and may inform the police or excise authorities. The guest is responsible for any legal action, arrest, fine or imprisonment under the Act.
  • The hotel is not responsible for any consequences faced by a guest who breaks this law.
  • Authorities may inspect premises as the law allows, and we will co-operate fully with them.
Thank you for helping keep Hotel Vijay a safe, peaceful family hotel for all guests.

Our Story

A Home in the Valley of Rajgir

Nestled in the serene valleys of Rajgir — one of India's most sacred and historically rich destinations — Hotel Vijay offers warm, comfortable hospitality at the heart of this ancient city.

Located beside the Ram Krishna Math on Hot Spring Road, our hotel sits at an exotic vantage point offering breathtaking views of sunsets and sunrises cradled in nature's lap. Whether you are a pilgrim, a history enthusiast, or a leisure traveller, we promise a stay that is simple, honest, and memorable.

With dedicated service and homelike warmth, Hotel Vijay has made every journey to Rajgir truly unforgettable — from the sacred hot springs to the Nalanda ruins and the Vishwa Shanti Stupa.

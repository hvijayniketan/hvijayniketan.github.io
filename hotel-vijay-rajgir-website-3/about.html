Our Story

A Home in the Valley of Rajgir

Nestled in Rajgir, Bihar, Hotel Vijay offers warm hospitality at the heart of this ancient city. We sit beside the Ram Krishna Math on Hot Spring Road, with lovely views of sunrise and sunset.

Pilgrims, history lovers, families and leisure travellers choose us as their hotel in Rajgir for simple, clean rooms and helpful service, close to the hot springs, Venuvan, Vishwa Shanti Stupa and the Nalanda ruins.

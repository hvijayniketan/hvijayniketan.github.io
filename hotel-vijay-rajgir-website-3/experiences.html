Things to Do in Rajgir

From the glass bridge to ancient Buddhist sites, plan your experiences from a hotel close to it all.

Walk on the Glass Bridge

Stand on an 85-foot glass skywalk above the valley at the Nature Safari.

Ride the Ropeway

Glide up to Vishwa Shanti Stupa for views of the Rajgir valley.

Dip in the Hot Springs

Visit the sacred Rajgir hot springs, 1 km from the hotel.

Wildlife and Nature Safari

Spot wildlife and enjoy the forest on a safari with the family.

Sunrise at the Peace Pagoda

Go early for soft light and quiet over the hills.

Day Trip to Nalanda

Walk through the UNESCO ruins of the ancient university.

Rajgir Mahotsav

Rajgir hosts a dance festival in December, a good reason to plan a winter trip.

Horse-cart Ride

Try the traditional tonga, still part of Rajgir's local transport.

Book a Room at Hotel Vijay, Rajgir

Affordable rooms, free parking and a restaurant, close to Rajgir hot springs, Venuvan and Vishwa Shanti Stupa.
